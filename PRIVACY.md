# 隐私政策 (Privacy Policy)

**生效日期：2026年5月17日**

您的隐私安全对我们至关重要。NVR 采用 **100% 本地化闭环架构**。我们向您郑重承诺：本软件未设立任何第三方云端服务器，绝对不收集、不缓存、不上传您的任何流媒体视频流、监控声音、录像文件或个人信息。所有数据流均在您的本地网络内直接与摄像头交互并本地存储。

---

### 一、 数据收集与使用
1. **监控视频与音频**：本软件不收集、不存储、不上传您的任何监控视频和音频。所有流媒体数据均在您的本地局域网内直接传输。
2. **设备凭证**：您在添加摄像头时输入的账号、密码或 IP 地址等敏感凭证，均直接存储在您的 iOS 本地沙盒或 Apple 系统 Keychain 中。这些数据仅用于本地连接鉴权，外界无任何途径获取。
3. **个人信息**：我们不收集您的姓名、电话、邮箱、设备 ID 等任何个人信息。

### 二、 系统权限调用
为了提供正常的监控与录像功能，本软件需要调用以下系统权限：
1. **局域网/本地网络 (Local Network)**：用于发现和连接您相同 Wi-Fi 下的摄像头设备。
2. **照片库权限 (Photo Library)**：当您保存截图或视频至系统相册时需要此权限。
3. **文件共享 (File Sharing)**：本软件支持通过系统“文件”App访问您本地保存的录像。

### 三、 第三方 SDK
本软件**未集成任何**广告追踪、行为数据统计分析等侵害隐私的第三方商业化 SDK，确保软件运行绝对纯净安全。

# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: May 17, 2026**

Your privacy and security are paramount to us. NVR adopts a **100% localized closed-loop architecture**. We solemnly promise: This software has no third-party cloud servers, and absolutely does not collect, cache, or upload any of your streaming video streams, monitoring sounds, recording files, or personal information. All data streams interact directly with cameras within your local network and are stored locally.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Use
1. **Video and Audio**: This software does not collect, store, or upload any of your surveillance video and audio. All streaming data is transmitted directly within your local network.
2. **Device Credentials**: Sensitive credentials such as accounts, passwords, or IP addresses entered by you are directly stored in your iOS local sandbox or Apple system Keychain. This data is only used for local connection authentication.
3. **Personal Information**: We do not collect any personal information such as your name, phone number, email, or device ID.

### 2. System Permissions
To provide normal functions, this software needs to call the following system permissions:
1. **Local Network**: Used to discover and connect camera devices under the same Wi-Fi.
2. **Photo Library**: Required when you save screenshots or videos to the system album.
3. **File Sharing**: This software supports accessing your locally saved recordings through the system "Files" app.

### 3. Third-party SDKs
This software **does not integrate any** third-party commercial SDKs such as advertising tracking and behavioral data statistics, ensuring that the software runs absolutely pure and safe.
