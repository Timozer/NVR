Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new camera to NVR?

NVR makes adding cameras incredibly simple. Ensure your camera device and your iOS/Mac device are connected to the same Wi-Fi or Local Area Network (LAN). Go to Settings, click "Discover Cameras", and NVR will automatically scan the network using the ONVIF protocol. If discovered, tap on it to add it instantly. Alternatively, you can add RTSP or ONVIF cameras manually by entering their network IP addresses, ports, and login credentials.

Why is my camera showing offline or failing to load?

This is usually due to network configuration or wrong credentials. Please check the following:

  • Verify your camera is powered on and connected to your local network.
  • Make sure local network permissions are allowed for the NVR app in your iOS Settings (Privacy & Security -> Local Network -> NVR).
  • Double-check the ONVIF or RTSP connection ports (ONVIF typically uses 80, 8000, or 8899, while RTSP stream uses 554).
  • Ensure the camera's username and password entered in NVR match its configuration.
Where are screenshots and recordings saved?

Screenshots and manually recorded video clips are saved directly to your system's Photo Library for quick sharing. The app will prompt for Photo Library access when you trigger these actions for the first time. For continuous video recordings or manual exports, they are saved locally in the app's sandboxed filesystem. You can easily view, share, or download them through the iOS native "Files" app under the "NVR" folder.

Are my cameras secure? Does NVR upload video streams?

Your privacy and stream security are our top priorities. NVR is a 100% localized application. We do not run, maintain, or upload any video streams or credentials to cloud servers. All stream connections, authentication, video rendering, and storing processes happen entirely on your local device. Your video streams never leave your own network, ensuring ultimate peace of mind.
